# Binding Operational Directive 26-04

The README of `cisagov/kev-data` says CISA's requirements for adding to the catalog rely on the authority of BOD 26-04. CISA's JSON schema names it in its description of `forensicTriage`.[^known_exploited_vulnerabilities_schema.json]

CISA's page for the directive answered a plain client with 403 on 2026-11-03. This project does not get round that, so it cites the page by address and date, and restates no term of the directive in the schema. Secondary notes read that day say BOD 26-04 was issued on 10 June 2026 and revokes and replaces BOD 22-01.[^cisa-bod-26-04-faq-vulnerability-remediation-impact]

Older text keeps the older name: in the pinned catalogue, `requiredAction` names BOD 22-01 on 340 entries and BOD 26-04 on 149.[^test_kev_data.py]
