# Policies

* [Security policy](security-policy.md) - How to report a vulnerability (GitHub's private vulnerability reporting), which release receives fixes (the latest), what executes in the repository and what holds each surface, what the upstream watch and the fetcher may reach, and the two repository settings not yet in force.
* [AI covenant](ai-covenant.md) - The rules for AI-assisted work here: a person owns every contribution, an element says what the upstream says, no rule of a model's own goes in the schema, no dated fact sits in an element, and the scheduled librarian's changes need a person's review.
* [Licence and attribution](license-and-attribution.md) - The terms here: Apache-2.0 for the schema and code, CC BY 4.0 for the bundle, and CC0 1.0 for CISA's copied files, with CISA's statement that use gives no right to the CISA Logo or the DHS Seal and is no endorsement. NOTICE holds the whole position.
